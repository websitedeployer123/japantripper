The Trip
in Plain English

A 60-day Japan workation from Tuesday Sep 15 to Friday Nov 13, 2026: 45 planned days plus 15 blank days left open for ideas we add later.

Start Big in TokyoArrive, recover, explore Shibuya/Akihabara/Odaiba, do the helicopter flight, eat omakase, and take the GT-R/RX-7 drive into Hakone.
Go North for Wild JapanNikko, Ginzan Onsen, Sendai, Matsushima, Naruko Gorge and the snow monkeys give the trip its mountain/onsen spine.
Then Culture + FoodKyoto, Nara, Osaka and Kōya-san carry the temples, sword class, street food, kaiseki, JJK/Naruto energy and night photography.
Westward PilgrimageHiroshima and Miyajima slow the pace down, then Shikoku adds Dōgo Onsen and Naruto whirlpools before returning through Ise.
Finish the Planned Route in HokkaidoSapporo, Daisetsuzan, Asahikawa, Lake Tōya, Noboribetsu and Otaru close the planned 45-day route.
Keep 15 Days BlankDays 46–60 are open placeholders for activities, reroutes, rest days or places you add later.

Autumn · Forests · Mountains · Coasts

Nature & Scenic
Highlights

Nature stops on the route, with rough autumn timing.

🍁 Peak: Mid–Late October
Daisetsuzan National Park
📍 Hokkaido
Large Hokkaido national park with volcanic peaks, alpine terrain, early autumn colour and the Asahidake Ropeway.
⏱ 2 full days minimum. Stay at Asahidake Onsen.
🌊 September Oyster Peak
Matsushima Bay Islands
📍 Miyagi, Tohoku
Boat tour through the bay's pine-covered islands, plus oysters on the pier if timing works.
⏱ Half-day or overnight from Sendai.
🍁 Peak: Early October
Naruko Onsen Gorge
📍 Osaki, Miyagi
Gorge walk, kokeshi workshops and an onsen town stop. Best timing is early October.
⏱ Full day. Combine with Sendai.
🌋 Year-round
Jigokudani Snow Monkey Park
📍 Yamanouchi, Nagano
Wild Japanese macaques bathing in natural volcanic hot springs. October brings cool air and morning steam — the monkeys are more active, the autumn leaves frame the pools. Arrive by 7am to miss coach tour groups. 30-minute forest hike to reach the springs.
⏱ Half day. Combine with Karuizawa.
🍁 Late October
Kōya-san Ancient Cedar Forest
📍 Wakayama
Ōkunoin approach, cedar trees, stone lanterns and temple lodging if the schedule allows.
⏱ Overnight preferred.
🌊 September / October
Ise-Shima National Park
📍 Mie Prefecture
Rocky peninsula, pearl farm bays, hidden coves. Ama women divers still free-dive for abalone in traditional white garments. Kayak the Ago Bay inlets. Fresh lobster and abalone at ama huts on the coast — some of Japan's most unique dining experiences.
⏱ 1–2 days from Ise City.
🌋 Year-round
Lake Tōya Volcanic Caldera
📍 Hokkaido
Volcanic caldera lake with Mt. Yōtei views. Combine with Noboribetsu on the same day if driving.
⏱ Half day. Combine with Noboribetsu.
🏝 September
Miyajima Island: Mt. Misen Forest
📍 Hiroshima Prefecture
Beyond the famous torii gate, Miyajima's Mt. Misen is covered in primeval forest that's been sacred since the 8th century. The summit (535m) gives views over the entire Seto Inland Sea. Ancient "sacred fire" burning since Kūkai lit it 1,200 years ago. Wild deer. No development. One of Japan's most quietly spiritual hikes.
⏱ Full day. Stay overnight on island.
🌲 September / October
Shiraito Falls, Karuizawa
📍 Nagano Prefecture
A curtain of 80 separate white streams cascading over a 70m basalt ledge — "white thread falls." Surrounded by forest that's just beginning to turn gold in late September. Famous enough to appear on old currency but still relatively easy to visit. Combine with the elegant Karuizawa resort town.
⏱ Half day from Karuizawa Station.

🎵 A Note on Nujabes

Jun Seba (瀬葉純) was born 1974 in Tokyo, died February 2010 in a traffic accident. His label was Hydeout Productions, based out of his Shibuya record shops.
He composed the entire soundtrack for Samurai Champloo (2004) — if you haven't watched it before this trip, do. It's set in Edo-period Japan with hip-hop sensibility and will reframe how you see Kyoto and Nara.
His two albums — Metaphorical Music (2003) and Modal Soul (2005) — are worth picking up on vinyl at Face Records. Originals are rare and expensive; reissues are available.
The word "nujabes" is his real name — Jun Seba — spelled backwards. A quiet, characteristic move from someone who preferred to stay in the shadows of his own music.

Logistics · Money · Transport · Timing

Essential Tips
& Logistics

Practical knowledge that saves time, money and frustration across 45 days, including the book-ahead ticket calendar.

🚅 Transport

Do a JR Pass vs individual tickets check once the route is final. The current budget assumes either a 21/14-day pass mix or individual Shinkansen tickets depending on final routing.
IC Card (Suica or Pasmo): load ¥5,000–10,000 and use it for metro, buses and convenience stores.
Shinkansen is the default for anything over 90 minutes. Overnight buses are cheap alternatives (¥3,000–6,000) for Osaka-Tokyo if you want to save a hotel night.
Taxis are expensive (~¥700 flag-fall). Use for short late-night journeys only. Uber works in major cities; Go app works everywhere.
For Hokkaido, rent a car in Sapporo for the Daisetsuzan + Lake Tōya + Noboribetsu loop.
Domestic flights: Tokyo–Sapporo is 1.5 hours by ANA/JAL. Book 2–3 months ahead for ¥6,000–12,000. Better than the 10h Shinkansen for Hokkaido.

💴 Money & Budget

Japan is still largely cash-heavy, especially in rural areas, temples and small restaurants. Always carry ¥10,000–20,000 in cash.
7-Eleven ATMs accept all international cards and dispense cash 24/7. This is your most reliable ATM source across all 45 days.
Mid-range daily budget (excluding big experiences and fine dining): ¥15,000–25,000/day covers food, transport, entry fees and accommodation.
No tipping in Japan. Pay the listed price and leave it there.
Ichiran ramen: easy solo meal, form ordering, around ¥1,000 plus extras.
100-yen shops (Daiso, Seria): useful for chopsticks, small containers, phone accessories and umbrellas.

🍁 Autumn Foliage Timing (Sep–Oct)

Mid-September: Hokkaido Daisetsuzan alpine zone peaks first. Visit early in trip if Hokkaido is planned at start.
Late September: Hokkaido lowlands, Naruko Onsen Gorge (Tohoku), Nikko forests. Plan days 11–18 here.
Early October: Jigokudani Monkey Park, Nagano Alps, Karuizawa. Days 17–18 hit this perfectly.
Mid October: Kyoto begins — Philosopher's Path, Arashiyama, Tōfuku-ji illuminations start. Days 19–28 timing is ideal.
Late October: Tokyo — Shinjuku Gyoen, Ueno Park begin turning. Your final Hokkaido phase catches peak Daisetsuzan colour again.
Check koyo.walkerplus.com (Japan autumn forecast site) 2 weeks ahead for exact timing adjustments.

🎟️ Long-Term Ticket Planning

Now / May 2026: Tokyo Legacy Half Marathon 2026 is the urgent decision. Race day is Sun Oct 18. Priority/general entry windows run in May–June, and the race forces a Tokyo weekend reroute if accepted.
June–July 2026: Lock accommodation for Kyoto, Ginzan Onsen, Kōya-san, Hakone ryokan and Hokkaido onsen nights. Small ryokan and temple stays sell out earlier than city hotels.
July 10, 2026: Target Ghibli Park tickets if adding Nagoya. Overseas tickets normally open on the 10th of each month at 14:00 JST, two months ahead.
July–August 2026: Book GT-R/RX-7 rental, helicopter sunset flight, private Iaido, photographer, and Tokyo omakase/kaiseki. These are the highest-risk reservation items.
August 2026: Book domestic Tokyo → Sapporo flight, Hokkaido rental car, and any premium dining that opens two months out.
2–8 weeks before: Book teamLab Borderless, Shibuya Sky sunset slot, Skytree, special temple night illuminations and smaller timed-entry museums.
Week of travel: Re-check autumn foliage forecasts, typhoon/weather risk, Google My Maps route order, and restaurant closure days before locking each city block.
